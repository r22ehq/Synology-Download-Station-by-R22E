Control Synology Download Station from your browser.

R22E Station puts your NAS downloads within reach—without switching to DSM every time. Add links and torrent files, watch progress, and manage tasks from a compact browser popup.

► Add downloads in seconds
Paste a file URL or magnet link, upload a torrent file, or send a downloadable link from the right-click menu. You can also collect download links from the current page.

► Keep track of your tasks
See download progress, transfer speeds, and task status. Filter tasks by activity or completion, expand task details, and pause, resume, or remove selected downloads.

► Choose where downloads go
Follow your NAS's Download Station destination by default, or save a custom folder for each NAS profile. Browse folders and override the destination when adding a download.

► Manage transfer limits
Adjust supported Download Station speed limits from Settings. These are NAS-wide settings and require a manager account; use 0 for unlimited.

► Make it your own
Choose light or dark mode, customize the accent color, and enable optional browser notifications. Pick from six completion sounds and preview them before saving.

► Connect to your own NAS
Use Local HTTPS with a certificate your browser trusts. Local HTTP is also available for trusted networks, but it is not encrypted. An experimental QuickConnect-ID option supports direct connections only; it is unofficial and may stop working. QuickConnect relay is not supported.

► Your NAS, your account
No R22E account is required. Access to your NAS or a selected source site is requested when needed. Saving your NAS password is optional; saved passwords stay in this browser's local extension storage, without extension-level encryption.

WHAT'S NEW IN 0.1.7
• Fixed an intermittent return to Login Required after Test connection and Save and connect.
• Prevented delayed session reads from overwriting a new login or restoring a session after logout.

GETTING STARTED
A compatible Synology NAS, Download Station, and your own NAS account are required. Open the extension, choose Add NAS, enter your DSM address, and connect.
